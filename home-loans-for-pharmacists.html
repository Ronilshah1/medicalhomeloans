What an LMI waiver is worth.

Lenders Mortgage Insurance normally applies above 80% of the property value. If you qualify for a waiver, that premium stays with you, and you can buy sooner with a smaller deposit.

FAQ

Home loans for pharmacists: common questions

Can pharmacists get a home loan without LMI?

With some lenders, yes. Several include AHPRA-registered pharmacists in their LMI waiver policy, usually with a minimum income requirement. We’ll tell you which lenders you qualify with.

Does my pharmacy intern year count?

Lenders generally want full registration before applying their professional policy, but we can plan your purchase timing around when you’ll qualify.

I own a share of a pharmacy. How is my income assessed?

Lenders look at your share of the business profits through your company or trust, with certain add-backs. We work with your accountant to present it clearly.
