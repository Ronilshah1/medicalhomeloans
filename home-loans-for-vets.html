What an LMI waiver is worth to a vet.

LMI is usually charged when you borrow more than 80% of a property’s value. Where a lender includes vets in its waiver policy, you keep that premium — often enough to cover your stamp duty or fund your offset account from day one.

FAQ

Home loans for vets: common questions

Do veterinarians qualify for an LMI waiver?

With some lenders, yes. Eligibility usually depends on registration with your state or territory veterinary board, and sometimes on a minimum income. Because policies differ widely, we confirm eligibility before you apply.

Do vet nurses qualify?

Vet nurses generally aren’t included in lenders’ professional LMI waivers, but we can still find lenders that count shift and overtime income generously and compare government schemes for first home buyers.

I work locum shifts across several clinics. Can I still get a home loan?

Yes. Some lenders assess contractor income on a shorter history than standard self-employed borrowers. We’ll present your income so it’s assessed fairly.
