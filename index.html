LMI waiver for medical professionals.

Did you know the banks offer home loans specifically for medical professionals? It’s the well-deserved reward for those who keep our communities thriving.

Medical professionals can access exclusive banking benefits, including waivers on Lenders Mortgage Insurance (LMI) for their home or investment property loans. LMI is a one-time premium, generally 1%-5% of the loan amount, that protects the bank should a borrower default. Meaning an LMI waiver on a $600,000 loan could save medical professionals between $6,000 and $30,000 in premiums.

If you're a medical professional with a standard home loan or investment property loan, now might be the perfect time to explore more rewarding borrowing options.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about securing an exclusive medical professional home loan.

Which medical professionals actually qualify for a Doctor mortgage loan with 95% LVR with no LMI?

The LMI waiver is available to a wide range of AHPRA-registered professionals. This typically includes General Practitioners, Medical Specialists, Surgeons, Dentists, Optometrists, Pharmacists, and Veterinarians. Even if you are currently a medical resident or registrar, depending on your income trajectory, we have access to lenders who will extend these exact same premium waivers to you.

A large part of my income comes from overtime, shift penalties, or locum work. Will banks accept this?

If you go to a standard branch, their automated systems will often penalise or heavily "shade" irregular income. However, because we specialise in medical lending, we know exactly which lenders will accept 100% of your hospital overtime, Medicare billings, private practice dividends, and locum contracts to accurately reflect your true borrowing capacity.

I have a significant HECS/HELP debt from my medical training. Will this ruin my borrowing capacity?

It is incredibly common for our clients to have large student debts, but it shouldn't lock you out of the premium property market. Standard lenders use harsh calculators that heavily reduce what you can borrow based on HECS debt. As an ex-banker, Ronil actively selects lenders with the most generous servicing calculators specifically designed for medical professionals, minimising the impact of your student debt.

How does a Doctor Mortgage Loan provide more borrowing power than a standard loan?

A specialised Doctor Mortgage Loan is designed around the high-earning potential of medicos. While standard banks might "shade" your overtime or locum income, these specific loan structures allow for 100% of your total medical income to be considered, often alongside an LMI waiver up to 95% LVR.

Are Medical Professional Home Loans only for General Practitioners?

No. Our Medical Professional Home Loans are available to a broad range of AHPRA-registered specialists, including Surgeons, Dentists, Veterinarians, and Optometrists. We ensure your specific profession's income nuances are matched with the right lender policy.
