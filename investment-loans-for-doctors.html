Medico benefits on investment loans.

  • LMI waivers. Some lenders extend their medico LMI waiver to investment properties, so you can invest with a smaller deposit.

  • Professional pricing. Medico discounts can apply to investment lending with some lenders, not just owner-occupied.

Structure matters more than rate.

How your loans are set up affects your cash flow, your flexibility and how easily your accountant can work with them. We think about:

  • Keeping loans separate for your home and each investment, rather than cross-securing properties.
  • Offset accounts in the right place, so your savings reduce the interest that matters most to you.
  • Interest-only versus principal and interest periods, weighed against your goals and lender rules.
  • Future borrowing capacity, so your next purchase isn’t capped by how you set up this one.

FAQ

Investment loans: common questions

Can doctors get an LMI waiver on an investment property?

With some lenders, yes. Others limit their medico waiver to owner-occupied homes. We’ll show you which lenders apply it to investment loans for your situation.

Can I use equity in my home to buy an investment property?

Often, yes. Many investors use a separate loan secured against their home’s equity to fund the deposit and costs, keeping each loan distinct.

Should I get tax advice before investing?

Yes. We handle the lending, and we recommend speaking with your accountant about tax, ownership structure and negative gearing before you buy.
